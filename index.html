fixmylinux

Fix your Linux in one command.

Codecs, video decoding, drivers and apps, sorted. Supported so far: Fedora.

1. Copy this command

curl -fsSL https://fixmylinux.dev/setup.sh | bash
  1. 2. Open the launcher Alt+Space or Alt + F2
  2. 3. Paste Ctrl+V
  3. 4. Run it in a terminal Shift+Enter

5. Type your password when it asks, then press Enter. You might see stars as you type, or nothing at all. Both are normal, so just keep typing.

Nothing happened when you pressed the keys? Use a terminal instead.

1. Copy this command

curl -fsSL https://fixmylinux.dev/setup.sh | bash
  1. 2. Open a terminal Ctrl+Alt+T GNOME: press Super, type “terminal”, press Enter
  2. 3. Paste Ctrl+Shift+V
  3. 4. Run it Enter

5. Type your password when it asks, then press Enter. You might see stars as you type, or nothing at all. Both are normal, so just keep typing.

What it does

  • Updates your system
  • Adds the RPM Fusion repositories
  • Installs full multimedia codecs, so videos and music just play
  • Turns on hardware video decoding for AMD and Intel graphics
  • Adds Flathub to your app store for thousands more apps
